4. Subscription plans

Kraken Guard is offered on three plans:

  • Free — filtering on one social media platform, 20 shared blocklist entries, no community suggestions
  • Pro (£3.99/month) — filtering on all 4 supported platforms, 30 blocked accounts and 30 blocked keywords per platform, no community suggestions
  • Pro+ (£8.99/month) — filtering on all 4 supported platforms, unlimited blocked accounts and keywords, community suggestions included

Plan features and pricing may change from time to time. We will give reasonable notice before any price increase applies to an existing subscription.
